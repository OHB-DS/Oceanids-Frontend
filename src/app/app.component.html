Welcome to the Oceanids Coastal Resilience Platform 🌊

Explore coastal indicators for each site, compare outputs interactively, and download ready-to-use figures for reports 📊

  • 📍 First select a site from the Location menu on the map.
  • 🧭 Then choose one or more data services: Coastal Change, Ground Motion, Wave Climate, Sea Level, Atmospheric Data, Coastal Flooding and Tidal Flats Mapping.
  • 🗺️ Click the available map features to visualise the outputs interactively and adjust filters where available.
  • ℹ️ Use the Information button to read more about each service and its methodology.
  • 📦 Download the raw data for further analysis as CSV, GeoJSON, or TIFF.
  • 🖼️ Download a JPG from the analysis panel when you want to include the output in a report.
